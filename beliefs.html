Our faith is rooted in Scripture

Beliefs that shape how we live, not just what we say.

Our Seventh-day Adventist faith is built on the Bible and centered on Jesus Christ. These beliefs shape more than how we worship on Sabbath morning. They guide how we love our families, treat our neighbors, care for our health and look toward the future with hope.

Whether you grew up in church or are exploring faith for the first time, we hope this page helps you understand who we are, and why it matters to us.

What we believe

Our Core Beliefs

Our faith is grounded in God’s Word and centered on Jesus. These five themes summarize what guides our worship, our relationships and our hope.

Belief 01: God and His Word

We believe the Bible is God’s inspired Word: a trustworthy guide for faith and life, and the standard by which every teaching is tested.

We worship one God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, three Persons united in love and working together for our salvation.

“Thy word is a lamp unto my feet, and a light unto my path.”

Psalm 119:105 (KJV)

What this means for you: You don’t have to take anyone’s word for it, including ours. Open the Bible, ask questions and discover God for yourself.

Read more
2 Timothy 3:16–17 · Matthew 28:19 · John 1:1–3
02

Belief 02: Creation and Humanity

God created the world in love and called it “very good.” Every person is made in His image and is worthy of dignity, respect and compassion.

We live in a world touched by the struggle between good and evil, but God has not given up on His creation. He is working to restore it to harmony.

“So God created man in his own image, in the image of God created he him; male and female created he them.”

Genesis 1:27 (KJV)

What this means for you: Your life has value because God made you on purpose. No past, background or mistake changes that.

Read more
Genesis 1–2 · Psalm 139:13–14 · Romans 8:19–22

At the center of our faith

Belief 03: Jesus and Salvation

At the center of everything we believe is Jesus Christ: His life, His death on the cross and His resurrection. Through Him we find forgiveness, grace and new life.

Salvation is God’s free gift. We don’t earn it; we receive it by faith, and it grows as we walk with Him each day.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.”

John 3:16 (KJV)

What this means for you: God’s love for you is not a reward for being good enough. It is a gift, offered to you today.

Read more
Ephesians 2:8–9 · Romans 5:8 · 2 Corinthians 5:17
04

Belief 04: The Church and Christian Living

The church is God’s family: a worldwide community where we worship, learn and serve together. We celebrate baptism and the Lord’s Supper, honor the Sabbath and care for one another.

Following Jesus touches every part of life, from how we treat our families and neighbors to how we care for our bodies, our time, our resources and God’s creation.

“And they continued stedfastly in the apostles’ doctrine and fellowship, and in breaking of bread, and in prayers.”

Acts 2:42 (KJV)

What this means for you: Faith is not meant to be lived alone. There is a place for you in this family, whatever your story.

Read more
Romans 6:3–4 · 1 Corinthians 6:19–20 · Hebrews 10:24–25
05

Belief 05: Our Future Hope

God continues to guide His people through the Holy Spirit, His Word and the gift of prophecy. Jesus ministers for us in the heavenly sanctuary, and He has promised to return.

When He comes, He will put an end to sin, suffering and death, and make all things new. That is the “advent” hope in our name.

“Looking for that blessed hope, and the glorious appearing of the great God and our Saviour Jesus Christ.”

Titus 2:13 (KJV)

What this means for you: Pain and loss do not get the last word. You can face today with real hope for tomorrow.

Read more
John 14:1–3 · 1 Thessalonians 4:16–17 · Revelation 21:1–4
The “Seventh-day” in our name

Why We Worship on Saturday

From Friday sunset to Saturday sunset, we keep the seventh-day Sabbath, just as Jesus did. It is God’s weekly gift of rest: a sacred pause to set aside work and worry, worship together and enjoy time with Him and with the people we love.

“Remember the sabbath day, to keep it holy.”Exodus 20:8 (KJV)

  • Rest

    A full day to step back from work, screens and stress, and let God renew body and soul.

  • Worship

    We gather for Bible study and worship, and Friday evening vespers welcome the Sabbath in.

  • Relationships

    Time for family, friends and fellowship, often around a shared meal after church.

  • Service

    Like Jesus, we use the Sabbath to do good: visiting, encouraging and helping others.

Common questions

New to Adventism?

Here are answers to some of the questions people ask us most.

Are Seventh-day Adventists Christians?

Yes. We are a Protestant Christian church. We believe in one God, Father, Son and Holy Spirit, and that salvation comes only through faith in Jesus Christ. The Bible is our only rule of faith and practice.

Why do you go to church on Saturday?

The Bible names the seventh day, Saturday, as the Sabbath that God blessed at Creation and included in the Ten Commandments. Jesus and His early followers kept it too. For us it is a joyful day of rest and worship, not a burden. You can read more in the Sabbath section above.

Do I have to be an Adventist to attend?

Not at all. Everyone is welcome: members, visitors, families and anyone simply curious about faith. Come as you are, ask questions and take your time. No one will pressure you.

Why do Adventists care so much about health?

We believe our bodies are a gift from God, so caring for them is part of how we honor Him. Many Adventists eat a vegetarian or plant-based diet and avoid alcohol and tobacco. It is a big reason we love hosting community events like our Vegan Food Festival.

How can I learn more or study the Bible with someone?

We would love to help. Join a Sabbath School class at 9:30 am on Saturday, explore the 28 Fundamental Beliefs (opens in a new tab), or contact us to ask about Bible studies.
